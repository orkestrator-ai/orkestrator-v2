00006 · Bring the transcript annotation layer onto tokens

To do Medium Color tokens Evidence 0 / 4 images

Problem

TranscriptAnnotationLayer.tsx builds its action pill (:290) and its numbered pins (:346, :358) from bg-blue-600 and bg-blue-500 with white text, a white 2px border and a bespoke shadow. They float over the transcript, so a shadow is allowed, but the fill should still be primary-fill and the white border is a one-off.

Do reach for semantic tokens (elevated, divider, selected, success, failure) before any raw ramp step or hex.

Approach

  1. Move the pill and the pins to bg-primary-fill with text-primary-foreground; hover to bg-primary-fill/85.
  2. Keep the shadow (these float over the work). Decide whether the white border stays as a legibility ring over arbitrary transcript content.
  3. Update the pin tail so it uses the same fill as the pin.
