00007 · Unify the compose bar frame across tabs

To do High Components Evidence 0 / 18 images

Problem

NativeComposeBar.tsx:193 is rounded-xl on bg-compose-surface. PipelineMessageComposer.tsx:381, ClaudeTmuxChatTab.parts.tsx:1204 and FeaturesView.tsx:998 are rounded-2xl on bg-input-surface: a 16px modal radius on a translucent surface the spec rejects for this bar. The same composer looks different in each tab, and transcript text can show through the translucent ones.

Frame: a 12px-radius card on the opaque compose-surface (not the translucent input-surface), 1px border at 70%, 12px padding, compose-dock shadow.
DESIGN.md, Components → Compose Bar

Find every instance

grep -rnE --include=*.tsx "rounded-2xl[^\"]*bg-input-surface[^\"]*p-3" apps/web/src

Approach

  1. Capture the native composer first. It is the reference, and its before and after images should be identical.
  2. Bring each other composer to rounded-xl, bg-compose-surface, 1px border at 70%, 12px padding, the compose-dock shadow and the focus border step (one neutral shade up, never blue).
  3. Check the dock background fade and width formula (min(calc(100% - 2rem), 56rem)) on each.
  4. Check the input limits (one to twelve 20px lines) where an input is shared.
